Meet Bre

Welcome!

Hi! I’m Bre and I’m certified to perform Microblading and Powder Brow Permanent Makeup techniques.

I’m very happy to help people find the best custom brows that are both special and beautiful! From natural-looking hair strokes, to a neat powdery makeup look, I can tailor my services to your needs.

My PMU art style utilizes the natural features of the eyebrows and face in order to create a stunning look that is unique to each person.

Have any questions about the PMU services I offer? Contact me! My goal is to help get you the eyebrows you want!
