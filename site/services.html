Microblade

$350

2 hrs 30 mins

Microblading is a PMU technique that utilizes a hand-held tool equipped with tiny needles that implement pigment into the skin to mimic thin hair strokes. This method achieves the most realistic and natural brow look of all the services. The first initial touch up is included with the price listed here.

Ombré/Powder Brow

$350

3 hrs

Ombré/Powder Brow is a PMU technique that utilizes a tattoo machine equipped with a needle to essentially tattoo the skin. Both styles are to mimic the appearance of powder/makeup. Ombré brow is a more natural alternative to powder brow because the pigment fades towards the fronts of the brows creating an Ombré look. For those who want a more even, filled-in makeup look, the powder brow is the way to go! Powder brows are also the best at covering old PMU. The first initial touchup is included with the price listed here.

Combination Brow

$375

3 hrs 30 mins

Combination Brow is a PMU technique that utilizes both ombré brow and microblading. First, an ombré brow is tattooed to achieve more pigment on the brow tails that fade towards the middle-front area of the brows. Then hair strokes are added only to the fronts of the brows until they meet with the ombré brow. This style is great for those with very scarce to no brow hair as it achieves a more filled-in look than traditional microblade. The first initial touchup is included with the price listed here.
